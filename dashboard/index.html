Empirical Findings on Multi-Task Hypotheses

Hypothesis B1 (Interference)

Under equal per-run environment budgets, conditioned policies (π(a|s, v*)) exhibit slight representational sharing loss relative to unconditioned specialists trained strictly on target i.

Hypothesis B2 (Specialist Specificity)

Specialists severely degrade when evaluated zero-shot on other targets (e.g. specialist trained on 1.0 m/s performs poorly at 4.0 m/s), proving high task specificity.

Hypothesis B3 (PPO vs SAC Multi-Task Gap)

SAC's replay buffer retains multi-modal transition mixtures across velocities, requiring sufficient capacity and warm-up to prevent value gradient interference.

Theoretical Foundations & PRD Requirements

Critical algorithmic mechanics verified in this codebase

01

Termination vs. Truncation in Experience Replay

Transitions cut short by the simulation time limit (truncation) retain valid continuation value. Storing done = (terminated ∨ truncated) would falsely zero-out bootstrap targets:

y = r + γ (1 - terminated) · Q_target(s', a')

Our replay buffer stores only terminated as the bootstrap inhibitor and guarantees insertion of the true next observation prior to environment reset.

02

SAC Squashed Gaussian Entropy Auto-Tuning

SAC squashes unbounded Gaussian action samples via tanh and applies a change-of-variables Jacobian correction to log-probabilities. Temperature α is dual-optimized to maintain target entropy:

L_α = -log(α) · [log π(a|s) + H_target]

Target entropy H_target is set canonically to -&dim;(A).

03

TD3 Target Policy Smoothing & Delay

TD3 enforces deterministic actor optimization. To prevent critic value peaks around policy approximations, clipped Gaussian noise is injected into target actions, and actor weight updates are delayed (k = 2):

a' = clip(π_targ(s') + clip(ε, -c, c), a_min, a_max)

Actor loss -E[Q1(s, π(s))] executes strictly on even critic iterations.

04

Honest Handling of Censored Thresholds

In standard benchmarks, runs failing to reach a threshold θ are frequently dropped or averaged as N_max, biasing sample efficiency claims.

reached_threshold = k / n • median(steps | reached)

Our reporting module records exact seed arrival counts (k/n) and calculates medians solely over seeds that verified arrival.
